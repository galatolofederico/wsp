THE OBSERVATION
The policy receives four scalar observations per environment step. Their
individual meanings are not given.

THE ACTIONS
The policy selects one of two discrete actions per step.

THE NETWORK
A PPO-trained actor-critic. A shared MLP trunk maps the observations through
two tanh hidden layers with 256 and 192 units. A policy head emits two action
logits, and a value head emits one scalar state value. PyTorch nn.Linear
weights are stored as [out_features, in_features], with one bias entry per
output. The checkpoint has 51,203 parameters in total.

The task is a low-dimensional control problem. Identify the specific
environment or control task from the weights. Do not assume the task from this
description alone.
